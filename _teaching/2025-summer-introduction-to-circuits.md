Supported the undergraduate course *Introduction to Circuits*, assisting with recitation sessions, office hours, and laboratory instruction.

As a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course *Introduction to Circuits*, I supported a cohort of over 90 students through weekly recitation sessions and regular office hours. My role focused on reinforcing fundamental concepts in circuit analysis and helping students develop systematic problem-solving strategies.

In addition to classroom instruction, I assisted in laboratory sessions by guiding students through hands-on experiments involving circuit design, measurement, and troubleshooting. Through this experience, I strengthened my ability to explain technical concepts clearly, adapt explanations to different learning styles, and support students in applying theoretical knowledge to practical engineering problems.
